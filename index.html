How it works

From request to bloom day

Book four to six weeks ahead for events and flower walls. Arrangements usually need two or three days.

  1. Send a request

    Tell us the date, the vibe and what you'd like. It takes about three minutes.

  2. Quick consult

    Kristina follows up with ideas, flower options and a quote that fits your budget.

  3. Design & deposit

    Approve the design and pay a deposit to hold your date.

  4. Bloom day

    Flowers arrive fresh, get set up on site, and are picked up when the party's over.
